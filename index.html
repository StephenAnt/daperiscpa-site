Transparency & Pricing

Tax & Accounting services are billed across three tiers depending on your needs. Compliant tiers are paid upfront when you sign your engagement letter; Proactive and Strategic are recurring monthly investments for those who want a year-round relationship with a CPA, not just a once-a-year filer. Final pricing depends on the complexity of your situation. The best way to get an accurate quote is to fill out the contact form and schedule a consultation.

What's the differences between the various service tiers?

We’ve designed three distinct tiers—Compliant, Proactive, and Strategic—so you only pay for the exact level of support you need. Whether you just need your taxes filed accurately or you want year-round strategic planning, we have you covered.

More importantly, keeping your bookkeeping tax strategy, and tax prep all under one roof prevents the classic headache of having a bookkeeper and a tax preparer who don't talk to each other. Plus, having a unified financial foundation makes things infinitely easier when we need to collaborate with your other trusted professionals, like a financial advisor or attorney. We make sure your entire financial picture works together perfectly.

What kind of clients do you work with?

We work with clients in a variety of industries. This includes, restaurants, real estate investors, solopreneurs, freelancers, bars, cafes, marketing professionals, doctors, and independent creatives.

If you have a business, equity compensation, rental income or multi-state taxation, we're an especially good fit. Maybe you have none of these and don't want the stress of preparing your own taxes. If that's you, schedule a strategy session and let's talk.

What happens on a strategy session?

We'll discuss your current tax situation and see if we're a good fit. It won't cost you a thing, and we'll make sure that you leave with some value regardless.

Do we have to be in New York City to work with Daperis CPA?

Not at all! As a 100% virtual CPA firm, we work with clients nationwide. Our engagements are completely paperless, and we communicate in whatever way works best for you—whether that's video chat, phone, text, or email. Once you sign up, you'll get access to a secure TaxDome portal where you can easily message us and upload documents from any device.

What's the difference between tax compliance and planning?

Think of compliance as looking in the rearview mirror — the accurate, timely preparation of returns based on past events. Planning is looking through the windshield: a strategic, forward-looking analysis designed to legally minimize your tax liability well before filing season. If you want planning, we offer monthly subscription services with year-round support.

Do we have to use QuickBooks Online?

If you need bookkeeping services for your business or sole proprietorship, you'll need a QuickBooks Online subscription. This lets us access your reports and handle your bookkeeping as efficiently as possible.

My books are behind. Is that a problem?

Not at all and it's a common scenario. Cleanup is billed separately from ongoing bookkeeping. You will be given a custom quote based on the condition of your books. Most catch-ups run one to three months of work depending on how far back and how many accounts. Once you're current, we move to monthly bookkeping.

How do you keep my information safe?

We use a secure online portal hosted by TaxDome, accessible via their mobile app or my website. All tax and related documents are uploaded to this portal, which is SOC 2 Type II compliant — a critical security framework for organizations that handle sensitive data.
