Business Entity Advisory & Setup

Get It Right From The Beginning

Choosing the right setup for your business is a big deal. It affects your personal risk, how you get paid, and of course, how much the IRS takes at the end of the year. Before you elect S-Corp status, let's chat.

At Daperis CPA, we help new business owners navigate the financial and tax maze of partnerships, S-corps, and LLCs so you can feel confident you're making the right move. We don't just set you up and leave; we build a custom tax strategy that grows with you, making sure you keep as much of your hard-earned profit as possible.
